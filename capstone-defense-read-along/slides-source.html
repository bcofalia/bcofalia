Scope and delimitations

Four rules that fix the scope

Defined by work

EPC, civil and balance-of-plant, installation, O&M. Not by technology.

Tagged by technology

Solar, wind, hydro, other. The evidence can be filtered later.

Solar and wind first

Priority examples. Other technologies carry less weight.

Scope-confirmed checkpoint

The roadmap is tailored once the firm’s project is defined.

No surveys and no interviews. Published and public sources only.

Methodology · RQ1 and RQ2

Stream A: literature review

StepWhat the study does
ReportingPRISMA 2020, with a protocol registered before screening
DatabasesScopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect

Methodology · RQ4

The automation priority matrix

Payoff

Output per person, cost and time gained

Effort

Capital, skills and change required

Readiness

Mature and available to a local contractor now

Policy fit

Helped or hindered by current policy

Each option is scored 1 to 5, and every score cites its evidence. A sensitivity check tests the ranking under different weights.

The output

Three phases, three gates

PHASE 1

Foundation

High payoff, low effort options

Gate: data is captured reliably and used in decisions

PHASE 2

Pilot

One or two site-level options on a single project

Gate: the pilot meets its productivity and cost targets

PHASE 3

Scale

Proven options rolled out across projects

Gate: core business performance has held steady

At each gate: go, hold or stop. Phase contents shown are placeholders until the matrix is scored.
