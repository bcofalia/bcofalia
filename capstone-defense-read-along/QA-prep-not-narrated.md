# Prepared answers for likely panel questions

These are rehearsal notes for the live defense. They are not narrated in the video.

Why no survey or interviews? The question is what the evidence supports, and that evidence already exists in published studies and policy. A review also avoids the bias of an owner surveying his own staff. Expert review supplies the outside check.

Is a literature review enough for a capstone? The review is one of two streams. The capstone’s contribution is the design step: the matrix and the gated roadmap, built for a real firm and reviewed by experts.

How do you handle being the owner? The evidence is external and appraised with standard tools, scores cite their sources, experts review the output, and my position is declared.

What if little is published on renewable construction? The first question covers construction automation in general, which is well studied. Thin areas are reported as limited evidence and shown separately in the matrix.

Why dynamic capabilities? It explains how a firm senses an opportunity, commits to it and reorganizes, which is the path from market signal to roadmap.

Who did the work? Any assistance with searching, screening or extraction is disclosed. I set the questions and criteria, verify the sample, decide the scores and defend the result.

Why is AI not the focus? The study covers automation a contractor can adopt in its operations. AI is included only where a study treats it as part of one of those options.
